Transferred behavior from `packages/primitives/tests/meaning.test.ts`, `namespace-write.test.ts`, `relation-write.test.ts`, and `reference-write.test.ts` includes explicit resolved/unresolved identity outcomes, no relation for unresolved identity, one decision per mention, rejection of invented candidates, endpoint type constraints, and duplicate relation handling. Adapt-specific tests add similar-but-distinct identities, atomic per-input deferral, revision pins, correction history, bounded traversal, cycles, learned relationship queryability after original input disposal, and rejection of copied irrelevant observation text.

Thro does not provide Adapt's graph traversal boundary. `traverseGraph` is Adapt-owned. It traverses committed learned edges regardless of whether the original text still exists. Record, depth, and serialization bounds preserve explicit unknown context; a missing committed relationship produces `insufficient_memory` rather than a source-evidence claim.

The recipe supports two context modes. `complete_snapshot` preserves the isolated in-memory contract. `bounded_store` calls one injected `GraphLearningContextCapability` after extraction with deduplicated endpoint mentions, revision pins, request-scoped input IDs, and supplied record and character limits. It validates the returned lens, revisions, endpoint types, candidate IDs, and per-mention completeness before identity resolution. Incomplete identity or graph context defers affected work and cannot authorize automatic identity creation.

`commitGraphRecipeResult` is the thin atomic persistence facade. It accepts settled `change` or `no_change` results, validates change revision pins and the compact graph patch, and passes caller-supplied learned history, changes, outbox records, and residual-work metadata to the Graph store's single commit operation. Observation text exists only in temporary pending work and is disposed when its work is settled.
