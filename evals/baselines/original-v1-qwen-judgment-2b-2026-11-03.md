# Independent manual judgment: Qwen3.5 2B, original v1 histories

**The comparison is mixed. V2 produces more useful behavior and developer answers than this v1 run, but does not consistently turn experience into a coherent, revised understanding.** Preference evolution is worse, coffee has gains and losses, and therapist memory stops changing long before the history ends. These findings do not establish sustained improvement in Adapt's defining quality: learning over time.

This is a fresh judgment of the saved evidence. Withdrawn reviews and old baselines were not used. No model runs or source changes were made.

## Basis and scope

The orienting question is whether understanding becomes more useful as experience arrives. Integration, revision, meaningful context, and grounded answers explain that judgment; they are not separate acceptance thresholds. More text, more retained facts, and fluent answers are not inherently better learning.

Product intent comes from the [README](/Users/amir/projects/adapt/adapt-memory-v2/README.md), [eval playbook](/Users/amir/projects/adapt/adapt-memory-v2/internal-docs/playbooks/evals-playbook.md), [prompting playbook](/Users/amir/projects/adapt/adapt-memory-v2/internal-docs/playbooks/prompting-playbook.md), and [prompt-design documentation](/Users/amir/projects/adapt/adapt-memory-v2/website/content/docs/prompt-design.md). In particular, Adapt describes revising conclusions while retaining meaningful history, and the prompting principles favor grounded understanding over accumulated fragments.

I read all five original histories, both arms' midpoint and final checkpoints and final memories, all saved query outputs (including coffee before/after), and the v1 synthesized trajectories. For v2, I checked delivery revisions, read-only SQLite `learning_history`/`memory_changes`, and selected wire requests/responses to establish how specific conclusions changed. V2's events do not contain equivalent synthesized memory payloads. SQLite history after restoration is not always a complete earlier trajectory; checkpoints and wire evidence fill the relevant gaps. Final memory equals its final checkpoint in every reviewed arm.

## What this evidence supports

The strongest relative gains are practical: v2 avoids v1's severe invented trauma account in behavior and retains useful developer context that v1 loses. Its coffee endpoint also integrates relevant later evidence. These are real improvements in the tested pipelines.

The central weakness remains learning coherence over time. Existing conclusions can be corrupted during unrelated updates, stale and current states coexist without interpretation, and therapist learning stalls while processing continues. Several good final patterns were stated explicitly in the source, so they cannot establish that the learner independently formed those patterns across experience.

This run supports a mixed qualitative judgment, with substantial relative gains and substantial longitudinal failures. It does not identify a universal winner or establish reliability outside these saved histories and configurations. Repetition variability, the effect of the different public query paths, and the causes of the observed memory failures remain unmeasured.
