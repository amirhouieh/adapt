# Gemini: quality of persistent learning over time

**V1 provides stronger evidence of sustained useful learning in these configured runs. V2 also develops meaningful conceptual understanding; it is not merely accumulating events. Its advantage is clearest in faithful coffee integration and some qualified behavioral updates, while context selection, compaction and terminal batch failures limit its longer trajectories.** This is a judgment of the tested model-plus-pipeline combinations, not intrinsic Gemini capability or a general ranking of Adapt versions.

The only quality criterion is whether persistent understanding becomes more useful over time. Integration, revision, grounding and meaningful context explain that judgment; they are not separate thresholds. Memory length, prose polish and retained-fact counts do not determine it. Query answers were neither collected nor used.

## What this says about model versus pipeline

These runs show the current v2 procedure can produce conceptual integration with Gemini. They do not support explaining its limitations solely as a weak-model problem: omitted relevant context, meaning-changing compaction and rejected whole batches are visible mechanisms. Conversely, speculative v1 interpretations show that coherent synthesis itself is not sufficient evidence of grounded learning.

The terminal records distinguish configured constraints: behavior has `finish_length` failures with wire `MAX_TOKENS`, while developer and therapist have `memory_capacity` failures. Atomic handling leaves previous committed understanding intact; terminal receipt coverage is not equivalent to learning all supplied material. These are observations of this generation policy and pipeline, not intrinsic model limits. The earlier artificial context declaration is excluded entirely from the semantic verdict.

Overall, v1 sustains useful learning more consistently in this comparison. V2 shows real strengths, but does not demonstrate better learning over time across these histories. No new quality score, perfection requirement or general reliability claim follows.
