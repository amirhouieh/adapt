# Brain judgment from learners' stored understanding

Updated after the user's clarification. The quality judgment concerns what exists inside learners' persistent understanding and how it develops. Query answers, confidence scores, neuron counts and successful actions are not measures of learning quality. This replaces the earlier report's answer-oriented and mechanics-oriented framing. No new model runs or production changes were made.

## Judgment of the saved Brain's understanding

**The saved Brain learns a useful recurring planning-versus-action pattern, but its text learners repeatedly turn it into an overconfident psychological story. More specialized learners have not clearly produced deeper, better-qualified understanding in this example.**

This is the older behavioral Brain with persisted model google/gemini-3.1-flash-lite-preview, not the recent Qwen runs or an assembled adaptive-v2 Brain. Its five user learners were inspected directly in their SQLite understanding tables; their evolution summaries and retained observations were also read. [Brain registry and persisted configuration](/Users/amir/projects/adapt/adapt-memory/evals/.data/behavior-brain.db).

| Learner | What is actually stored | Judgment of that understanding |
|---|---|---|
| Avoidance patterns | “Preparatory Paralysis”: excessive research, administrative displacement, career/health avoidance and nocturnal consumption. | Useful connection across activities, but compresses varied actions into one avoidance explanation and asserts feeling safer as a cause. |
| Emotional state | Career anxiety, freezing, aggression toward Dave, and planning as coping; positive momentum “only” through rigid external accountability or micro-tasks against “baseline helplessness.” | Retains meaningful emotional context, but makes strong causal and absolute claims beyond the observed episodes. |
| Browsing analytics | Time-of-day differences, fragmented deep research, and a “Nocturnal Compulsive Reconstitution” / “Synthesis-Action Block” model. | Some distinctive temporal understanding survives, but elaborate labels and inferred motives overshadow concrete, qualified browsing patterns. |
| Intent/action gap | “Performative stagnation,” identity protection, and an executive-function deficit where intent “never initiates action.” | Recognizes a real recurring difficulty, but converts a pattern into an absolute account and asserted psychological mechanism. |
| Recurring interests | Separate records for ML, career transition, promotion, developer advocacy, watches, coffee and other topics, with updates/merges. | More concrete subject tracking; some persistence and consolidation are visible, but touch counts and topic inventory alone do not establish depth of understanding. |

The four text learners have different emphases, but their final understandings substantially converge on the same avoidance/inaction interpretation. Shared themes can be appropriate; the weakness here is that each adds certainty and inferred motives without adequately retaining the qualifications that distinguish the domains. For example, the emotional learner retains an observation of relief after scheduling a growth conversation, while the intent/action learner interprets that same step as rationalization and ends with “intent never initiates action.” That reveals how partial progress can be absorbed into a fixed negative theory rather than kept as a meaningful qualification.

The original corpus also contains attended gym sessions, course progress and partial manager engagement. A good final understanding would distinguish limited progress from continued avoidance. The saved final narratives largely emphasize paralysis. However, the complete original delivery trace and full understanding at every historical checkpoint are not available in this inspected Brain artifact; the retained observations are already model-derived. We cannot pin every omission to routing, observation, or synthesis, or claim a full reconstructed trajectory. [Original behavioral source](/Users/amir/projects/adapt/adapt-memory/evals/datasets/personal-behavior.json).

## What the stored evidence says about structural evolution

The behavioral Brain's own evolution table has zero recorded decisions and automatic evaluation was disabled. Its learner-level evolution rows summarize changes in understanding, not create/split/merge. We can judge the resulting learners' understanding as above, but cannot attribute it to structural evolution.

In a different older LFM2.5 1.2B run, a newly created learner starts with empty understanding and develops a useful stored summary connecting type-inference, negative numeric constant, declaration-emit and tuple-normalization issues. That is one concrete example of useful new understanding after coverage creation. The claim that those issues “intensified” is an interpretation rather than an established trend. There is no comparable later trajectory establishing that the reorganization improves sustained learning. [Stored new-learner understanding](/Users/amir/projects/adapt/adapt-memory/evals/.data/attention-response/2026-09-29-baseline/uncovered-complete/kebab-case-001.understanding.json); [Recorded empty-to-learned transition](/Users/amir/projects/adapt/adapt-memory/evals/.data/attention-response/2026-09-29-baseline/uncovered-complete/raw.jsonl).

The newer v2 activation/recovery fixtures preserve a prewritten approval preference unchanged. That is continuity evidence, not evidence of semantically improving understanding.
